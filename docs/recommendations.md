# Recommendations for the Head of Payments

Payments Optimization on 2025 data. All values are per year unless marked one-off.

## Bottom Line

The platform kept €42,594 on €11.5 million of sales in 2025, about €0.37 per €100. The recommendations start with options that are least costly and depend only on evidence: claim €17,357 from the banks, reprice the four shops that lose the platform money, and route Dutch, German and French cards to their domestic bank at the 16 shops where it pays.

No changes to 3D Secure yet, as the options need to be weighed: checking every payment would save the platform up to €18,002 a year in fraud, but it would cost the shops about €215,000 of sales and weaken the platform’s main selling point. I would recommend further cross-analysis into this decision with the available marketing data. The reasoning will be explained in detail below.

## Actions, In Order

| # | Action | Platform | Shops | Depends on |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Claim back missing payouts and wrong fees | €17,357 owed (one-off) | — | the banks accepting the evidence |
| 2 | Reprice S12, S16, S19 and S01 to the safe fee | +€6,068 | €0 | the shops accepting the new price |
| 3 | Route Dutch, German and French cards to their own country's bank (16 shops) | +€5,948 | +€144,378 | the new bank accepting the shop |
| 4 | Switch on automatic retries at the 6 shops without them, after step 2 | +€248 | +€44,639 | nothing outside the platform |
| 5 | Decide on 3D Secure (see below) | +€4,984 to +€18,002 | −€214,555 | marketing data |

**1. Claim back what the banks owe.** Four payouts never arrived (71 sales, €17,267), and Bank NL took €89.79 too much in fees on 96 payouts. Send each bank a short list of the affected days, with the expected and the reported amounts. Most of the €17,267 is money the platform owes its shops, so if the shops have already been paid, it is currently coming out of the platform’s own pocket. Run the payout check every month, and raise the repeated fee errors with Bank NL.

**2. Reprice the four loss-making shops.** S12 (−€1,781 in 2025), S16 (−€701), S19 (−€545) and S01 (−€438) pay less than their bank fees plus the expected fraud cost of their business type. The safe fee still breaks even if fraud runs high: S12 from 1.50% to 2.28%, S16 from 1.50% to 4.53%, S19 from 0.90% to 1.15%, and S01 from 1.10% to 1.20%. Present it openly, with each shop’s costs. S12 and S16 are still on the 2023 launch price, which no longer covers their costs. S01 and S19 are large shops on negotiated discounts, so their talks need more care. If a shop refuses, letting it go still raises platform profit by the amount it loses today.

**3. Route cards to their own country’s bank.** A Dutch, German or French card is approved 1.2 to 3.6 percentage points more often by its own country’s bank. At 16 shops this gains the platform money and wins the shops more sales. At the other 8 it costs the platform a little, because German cards would move to Bank DE, which charges more, so leave those shops as they are. Expect pushback from the bank that loses the cards, especially Bank DE, which signed many travel and subscription shops through a sales deal in 2023.

**4. Switch on retries at the remaining 6 shops.** Retries add little platform profit but win the shops €44,639 of sales, at no extra cost. The platform retries at most twice and only on “try again” declines, well within the Visa and Mastercard retry limits. Switch them on at S12 and S19 only after repricing, because each extra sale there loses the platform money until then.

## The 3D Secure Options

The fraud guarantee cost the platform €11,700 in reported fraud in 2025, and about €12,614 once late reports are counted. Checking every payment with 3D Secure would remove most of that cost: the platform would gain €4,984 (low estimate), €11,494 (best estimate) or €18,002 (high estimate). But customers would give up at the check, and the shops would lose €214,555 of sales, about €19 for every €1 the platform saves.

This is a marketing question: what is the cheapest way to win and keep shops?

- **Option A:** keep carrying the fraud cost as the price of “fraud protection included”.
- **Option B:** stop carrying it, and spend the money saved on other ways of winning or replacing shops.

To decide, we would need answers to the following:

- How many shops would stay without the guarantee?
- What would it cost to win the same shop sales (about €215,000) through other marketing?
- How does that cost compare with the €5,000 to €18,000 a year that checking every payment would save?

**A more conservative option worth testing first is checking every payment only at digital subscription shops, which have the highest fraud (€1.90 per €100 of sales), and at S14, which has about twice the fraud its business type predicts.**

## Limitations

- The fixes are ranked by their low estimate, so the ranking is cautious. A check against the true values showed that 3D Secure on every payment is worth about €14,443 a year, more than repricing.
- Fraud per shop is the weakest number: fraud rates are measured per business type, so shops like S14 can be off.
- The values come from one year of data and assume next year behaves similarly.
- The values can’t be added together, because some lost sales could be won back by more than one fix.

## Recommended Follow-Ups

These are the considerations I would recommend post-rollout:

- Bank Payouts: track that the next month's payout check shows no errors and exceptions.
- Shop Repricing: the four shops' monthly margins turn positive.
- Card Localization Routing: the approval rate of Dutch, German and French cards at the 16 implemented shops rises meaningfully.
- Automated Retries: the share of "rescued" payment outcomes rise at the 6 shops.
- High Fraud Flag: further inspect and review S14's abnormally high fraud levels.
